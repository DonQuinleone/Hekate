---
title: Guests
description: How people outside the workspace review without an account.
---

A **guest** reviews without an account: a client, a conductor, a label's A&R. They are invited by email to a review and open it through a link.

- **One link, for one person.** The link works once, opens a session in that browser, and leads only to the reviews they were asked into. It lasts three days unless the workspace chooses 14 or 30, and sending a new one withdraws the last.
- **A code as well, if the workspace asks.** Opening the link then sends a six-digit code to the address the review was sent to, and the review opens only when the code is typed. A forwarded email is then no use to whoever it was forwarded to.
- **What they can do** follows the review: listen and watch, comment, and decide where the review asks them to.
- **Ending access.** A guest's session lasts a fortnight, and ends sooner when the review closes or their link is revoked. Workspace admins see every guest and their links under Settings, Members.

Every link sent, every sign-in and every code tried is in the workspace's access log.
