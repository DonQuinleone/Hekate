---
title: Listen together
description: How one member leads playback for everyone listening along, from anywhere.
---

**Listen together** lets one member of a project lead a listening room: everyone who joins hears what the leader plays, as they play it. Press play, pause, seek, change the speed or open another version, variation or file of the project, and every listener's player does the same. It suits a remote session with a conductor, a producer talking a client through a mix, or an editor walking a director through a cut.

- **Starting one.** On a recording or a video, choose *Listen together* in the page's header, then *Start leading*. Starting another room ends the one you lead.
- **Inviting people.** Members of the project see the room under *Live in this project* in the same place, on any of its recordings, and join with *Join*. *Copy link* gives a link to send them; it opens a page saying who leads and what they play, with *Join*.
- **Listening.** Your player follows the leader's, through your own stream: your volume stays yours, and you hear only what you may hear yourself. If you play, pause or seek yourself, you stop following until you choose *Follow again*. When your browser wants a tap before it plays, *Start listening* is that tap. Elsewhere in the app, a pill at the bottom of the window takes you back to the room.
- **Who is there.** The leader sees who is listening. A page that has been closed leaves the room within a minute.
- **Ending.** The leader ends the room for everyone with *End for everyone*; a listener leaves with *Leave*. A room also ends when its leader has been away for a minute and a half.

Only members of the project can join, on their own session; guests, API tokens and members of other workspaces cannot. Each listener's playback is protected, watermarked and logged as if they had opened the file themselves.
